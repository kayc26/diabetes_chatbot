Insulin
Insulin is the key treatment for type 1 diabetes. It helps glucose move from your blood into your body's cells for 
energy. But not all types of insulin work the same way. Some offer steady coverage all day and night, while
 
others 
work quickly to handle blood glucose levels rising from meals or to bring high levels back to your target range. 
Your
 
healthcare team is the resource that will guide you to the right types and amounts of insulin for your
 
needs.

No single method is best for everyone. You’ll likely use a combination of these options as you find out what 
fits your life best at any given time. The key is to choose something that feels like you can stick to, fits your 
routine, and keeps your blood glucose in your target range. It’s okay to change how you take your insulin as 
technology advances or you go through life. You and your healthcare team will work together to choose what 
matches your needs.
