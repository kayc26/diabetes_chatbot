Living with type 1 diabetes means being ready for changes in your blood glucose levels. Many days feel steady, 
but unpla
nned changes to your day, such as illness, missed meals, extra physical activity, stress, or problems 
with the devices you use to manage diabetes, can sometimes lead to blood glucose levels outside of your target 
range. A few simple steps can help you adjust and manage with confidence.
