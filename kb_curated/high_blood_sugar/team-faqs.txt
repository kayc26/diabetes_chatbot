Q: What do I do when I have a high blood sugar (>250 mg/dL)?
A: Drink water, follow your provider's instructions regarding diabetes medications or correction insulin if you have not taken insulin in the last 4 hours, and monitor your blood sugar. If your blood sugar remains very high, you are vomiting, have stomach pain, difficulty breathing, confusion, or positive ketones, seek medical attention immediately.

Q: Why is my blood sugar high?
A: Common causes include eating more carbohydrates than usual, missed medications, illness, stress, lack of physical activity, poor sleep, or problems with insulin delivery devices. Tracking blood sugars, meals, medications, and routines may help identify patterns.
