Sick-day planning

Preparing in advance and knowing how to care for yourself when you are sick can help you
manage your food, fluids, and medications. When you are sick, blood glucose levels can
rise, even if you are eating less. Here are some suggestions:

- Check your blood glucose at least every four hours or as suggested by your health care
  team.
- Keep taking your diabetes medications, including insulin. You may need to adjust the
  dose based on your blood glucose results.
- Drink plenty of fluids to prevent dehydration. If you are unable to eat, you may need
  to replace your usual meals with carbohydrate-containing fluids.

Highs and lows can happen with changes in your living situation, food, activity, or
medications, so regular monitoring and support from your health care team will help you
stay healthy.
